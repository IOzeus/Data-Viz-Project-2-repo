# FIT3179 Data Visualisation 2 - Global Stock Market Performance

Interactive data visualisation exploring how the world's stock markets compare in size, growth and risk, how they move together, and where Australia fits in.

**Live site:** `https://iozeus.github.io/Data-Viz-Project-2-repo/`

## Data Sources
1. **World Bank, World Development Indicators**: market capitalisation of listed domestic companies (% of GDP and current US$), 1990–2024
2. **IMF, Monetary and Financial Statistics**: share price indices (Financial Market Prices) and monetary policy-related interest rates (Interest Rates)

`data/share-price-index.csv` and `data/policy-rates.csv` are extracts of the IMF downloads. The raw IMF files are too large for GitHub and are not committed.

## Tools
- **Vega-Lite** for all maps and charts (specs in `js/vega-specs/`)
- **GitHub Pages** for hosting
- **Google Fonts**
